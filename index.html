      About

      Who are we?

      Cerita Nyata is a student-founded podcast and short film initiative telling real stories responsibly. Guided by consent, respect, and authenticity, we amplify real voices without exploitation and use media to build understanding.

      What we’re building?

      We’re creating podcasts and short films based on real experiences. Through collaboration, we want to talk about real issues, start conversations, and share real struggles.

      What guides us

      Three promises we make to every storyteller.

      01

      Consent

      Every story is shared with permission. The storyteller decides what is told and how.

      02

      Respect

      The people behind the stories come first, before views, likes, or drama.

      03

      Authenticity

      We keep stories honest. No exaggeration, no staging, no sensationalism.

      Our CAS journey

      From an idea to stories people hear.

      Cerita Nyata is our CAS project. It follows the five CAS stages, and the line below fills in as you scroll through them.

      1. Stage 1

        Investigation

        Finding the issues that matter to the people around us, and the people who want their stories heard.

      2. Stage 2

        Preparation

        Planning episodes and films, agreeing on how we ask for consent, and learning to record, film, and edit.

      3. Stage 3

        Action

        Recording podcast conversations and shooting short films with our storytellers.

      4. Stage 4

        Reflection

        Looking back after every episode: what worked, what didn’t, and whether we treated each storyteller fairly.

      5. Stage 5

        Demonstration

        Releasing the stories and sharing what we learned with our school and the wider community.

      CAS learning outcomes

      What this project lets us show.

      • LO1

        Identify own strengths and develop areas for growth

        Each of us takes on roles, from hosting to filming to editing, that use our strengths and stretch our weak spots.

      • LO2

        Demonstrate that challenges have been undertaken, developing new skills in the process

        Learning new skills: interviewing, recording audio, filming, and editing.

      • LO3

        Demonstrate how to initiate and plan a CAS experience

        Cerita Nyata is student-founded. We started it, we plan it, and we run it.

      • LO4

        Show commitment to and perseverance in CAS experiences

        Committing to a series of episodes and films over time, not a one-off event.

      • LO5

        Demonstrate the skills and recognize the benefits of working collaboratively

        Everything we make is a collaboration, within our team and with the people whose stories we tell.

      • LO6

        Demonstrate engagement with issues of global significance

        Our stories deal with real issues and real struggles, and aim to start conversations about them.

      • LO7

        Recognize and consider the ethics of choices and actions

        Consent comes first. We amplify real voices without exploiting them.
